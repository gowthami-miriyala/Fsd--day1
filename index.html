About Collegee:

Aditya University is a State Private University located at Surampalem in Kakinada District, Andhra Pradesh. Established under the Andhra Pradesh Private Universities Act, 2016, the university has evolved from the well-known Aditya Engineering College, which was founded in 2001 under the Aditya Academy educational group.

History of Aditya

Aditya Engineering College (AEC) was established in the year 2001 by Aditya Academy. Rooted deeply in the vision of Dr. N. Sesha Reddy, The Aditya Academy, founded in 1984, with a vision and mission to create a platform for holistic growth and success to students. As a non-profit Educational Society, its commitment to quality education for rural students in Andhra Pradesh is a story of inspiration and perseverance. The Aditya educational group is with 80,000+ students in 80+ institutions with 8000+ staff across the coastal districts in Andhra Pradesh has become the standard bearer for quality education. In every stream, Aditya has become a springboard for success through its powered vision, constant innovation and professional excellence.
